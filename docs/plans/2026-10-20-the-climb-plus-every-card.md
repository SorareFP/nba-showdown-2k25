# The Climb: a "+" for every card

**DRAFT 2026-10-20, awaiting the user's approval** (the strategy card approval
rule). The user: "Can we now move on to adding + versions of every card in the
climb?" Thirty cards already have an approved "+" (the kits and the four kit
additions, docs/plans/2026-10-18-the-climb-upgrades.md). These are the other
**79**: every card a run can offer (no promo; the four legendaries are never
offered) plus the eleven Climb-only cards.

**The rule is the approved one:** a "+" is clearly worth a Rest Day — about
half again to double the card's main number, or the card plus one rider the
game already has (an Assist, a card drawn, 🔒 Uncancelable, a drawback removed,
a cheaper cost, a looser condition). No new mechanics, no new targets. Changes
are in **bold**.

**The strongest, worth a second look:** Switch Everything+ and Double Team+
(their drawback is gone), Ice Bath+ (it stays in the deck), Iso-Heavy+ (no
penalty to the other four), Fresh Legs+ (8 minutes is a full rest at the
usual tracker), Defensive Identity+ (+4/+4 to all five).

One thing noticed on the way: two cards print the same name, **Extra Pass**
(`extra_pass`, spend 2 Assists for any check, and `find_the_open_man`, +4 when
the opponent's Double Team leaves a player open). The second used to be "Find
the Open Man"; it may want a name of its own.

## When approved

Each "+" is built the way the first thirty were: the effect reads `plus` at
the card's door (execCard / canPlay / the AI / the board), the text goes in
strats.js PLUS, the faces export to public/cards/strats-plus/ (the card's own
art with the gold "+"), and the Practice screen offers them. A card with a "+"
can then be practised at a Rest Day, chosen by Film Study, and dealt as a "+"
copy where the Climb deals one.
