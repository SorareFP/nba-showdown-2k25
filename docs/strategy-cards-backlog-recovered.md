# Strategy Cards — the backlog, recovered from a damaged .docx

**The source file is corrupt.** `OneDrive/Documents/NBA Showdown 2K25 Strategy
Cards-DESKTOP-VFORBTS.docx` is truncated: 26,286 bytes ending mid-filename,
with no end-of-central-directory record, so no zip reader — Word included —
will open it. `word/document.xml` was still intact inside it, and this file is
its text, recovered 2026-09-07 by walking the local file headers and inflating
that one entry.

**Nothing here is derived, guessed or reworded.** It is the document's own
paragraphs in the document's own order, with the Word markup stripped. Where a
card reads oddly, that is how it reads in the source.

This is the backlog wave one (2026-09-06) was built from and wave two is still
to come. It lives in the repo now because the file it came from may not survive
another sync, and because a backlog nobody can open is not a backlog.

**Symbols, as the document uses them:** ✅ a card that is in; 🔒 cannot be
cancelled once played; ⚠️ a timing restriction; 🚫 a thing the card explicitly
does not do.

NBA Showdown 2K25 Strategy Cards:
Strategy cards:1. 3PT & Shooting-Based Cards
✅ "Heat Check" (Updated From Clutch Shot) 🔒
If a player reaches their highest scoring tier, attempt a 3PT shot check at -2.
If successful, gain +3 points and a hot marker.
🔒 Cannot be canceled once played.
✅ "Green Light" (New) 🚫
Choose one player. They may take three 3PT shot checks instead of their normal roll.
🚫 Cannot override defensive contesting strategy cards.
✅ "From Way Downtown" (Updated From Original) 🚫
Player makes a 3PT shot check at +1.
If the roll is 1-3, they get a cold marker.
If the roll is 18-20, they get a hot marker.
🚫 Can still be affected by defensive contest cards (e.g., "Close Out").
✅ "You Stand Over There" (New) ⚠️
Instead of rolling for a scoring phase, this player takes two 3PT shot checks.
⚠️ Must be played before rolling begins.
✅ "Catch & Shoot" (New) 🚫
If this player has Speed 14+, take a three-point shot check at +2. If successful, gain an assist.
🚫 Does not override defensive shot contesting cards already in play.
✅ "Outside Pick" (Updated) ⚠️
Discard a card to play.
This player makes a 3PT shot check at +5.
If successful, they score 3 points and a teammate gains +1 Assist.
⚠️ Must be played before a defender plays a contesting strategy.
2. Inside Scoring & Power Moves
✅ "Bully Ball" (New) 🔒
Play during the scoring phase.
Play on an offensive player with a power advantage. Make two paint shot checks. If their power advantage is 4+, they gain +2 to paint scoring rolls for four minutes.
✅ "Power Move" (Updated) 🚫
Player gets +2 Power for this scoring phase.
If their Power exceeds the defender’s Power by 5+ before this card was played, they gain +3 instead.
🚫 Does not override already-played defensive counterplay.
✅ "And One!" (Updated) 🔒
Play on a player with a speed or power advantage of 3+. Player scores 1 additional point. If their speed or power advantage is 5+, make an additional free throw shot check,
🔒 Once played, this cannot be negated.
✅ "Rimshaker" (Updated) 🚫
If a player has Power 15+ and a hot marker, they score 2 points and gain another hot marker.
🚫 Cannot cancel defensive contesting cards already played.
✅ "Drive the Lane" (Updated) ⚠️
If a player’s Speed is higher than their defender’s Speed, they take 2 free throws.
If they win the Speed matchup by 5 or more, the defender gets a cold marker.
⚠️ Must be played before any defensive foul-prevention cards.
✅ "Layup" (Updated)
If a player has 2 more Speed and 2 more Power than their defender, they automatically score 2 points.
✅ "Back to the Basket" (New)
Play during the scoring phase.
If a player has Power 13+ and a Paint Bonus, make a paint shot check.
✅ "Putback Dunk" (New) ⚠️
If a player has Power 14+ and the team leads in rebounds, they score 2 points automatically.
✅ "Inside-Out" (New)
If a player scores in the Paint, a teammate gets a free 3PT shot check.
🚫 Cannot override a previously applied "Slow the Pace."
✅ "Find the Open Man" (Updated) 🚫
If the opposing player is double-teaming someone else, their matchup gets +4 to their scoring roll.
🚫 Does not override already-played defensive cards.
✅ "Pick-and-Roll Maestro" (New) 🔒
If a player has Speed 14+, they may swap their defender with a different one.
If the new defender’s Speed is 5+ lower, make a shot check. If successful, gain 2 points and an assist.
🔒 Once played, this cannot be canceled.
✅ "Lob City" (New)
If a player has Speed or Power 15+, discard a card to play.
All your offensive players on the floor with 15+ speed gain an assist. All your offensive on the floor players with 15+ power score 2 points.
4. Defensive Stoppers & Matchup Control
✅ "Switch Everything" (Updated) 🔒
Defense can freely swap matchups once during the scoring phase. All offensive advantages are doubled for 4 minutes.
✅ "Defensive Stopper" (Updated)
If this player was on the bench last period, they gain +5 Speed and +5 Power on defense. Any defensive advantages gained by way of this card are capped at 0, and unnatural advantages are gained.
✅ "Anticipate the Pass" (Updated)
If the opposing team has 6 or more assists, spend an assist to take 2 assists from your opponent.
✅ "Turnover" (Updated) ⚠️
Play during the scoring phase
If the offensive player has any cold markers, draw 2 cards.
✅ "This Is My House!" (Updated) 🔒
If this player has higher Speed & Power than their matchup, skip the opposing player’s scoring roll for this four-minute period.
🔒 Once played, this cannot be negated.
3. New Team Identity & Playstyle Cards
✅ "Run the Floor" (Updated)
If 3+ players on the court have Speed 12+, your opponent allocates two paint shot checks among those players at +2. Gain an assist for each successful check.
Repeat each scoring phase until at least one of these players goes to the bench
✅ "Twin Towers" (Updated)
If 2+ players on the court have Power 14, your opponent allocates two paint shot checks among those players at +2. All your opponent’s paint shot checks are taken at -2.
This card stays in play until at least one of these players goes to the bench
✅ "Three-Point Barrage" (Updated)
If 3+ players on the court have a 3PT bonus, each player takes a three-point shot check. You can choose to spend one assist for one additional three-point shot check this scoring period.
✅ "Iso-Heavy Offense" (Updated)
Choose one player who takes over:
That player gets +3 to their scoring roll for four minutes.
All teammates suffer -2 to their own rolls.
✅ "Stretch Five" (Updated)
If a Center or Power Forward in play converts 3PT shot checks at 14-or-lower:
Take a three-point shot check with that player
Choose a teammate to take a paint shot check at +2
✅ "Post Domination" (Updated)
Play before the scoring phase.
If two players in play have a 15+ power, choose one of them and double their rebounds for four minutes.
✅ "Defensive Identity" (Updated)
If three or more players on the court have a defensive bonus, all teammates in play get +2 speed and power on defense for four minutes.
Creative Use of Accumulated Rebounds and Assists
15. Crash and Kick (Scoring Phase)
Spend 3 Rebounds and 1 Assist.
Your scoring player may attempt a 3PT Shot Check at +2.
16. Putback Specialist (Reaction Card)
After your player misses any shot check, spend 2 Rebounds to make another Paint Shot Check with any player in play at +3.
17. Extra Pass (Scoring Phase)
Play before a shot check. Spend 2 Assists. Make a shot check of any kind with any player in play. Shot check bonuses from other strategy cards are negated for this check.
19. Pick-and-Pop (Scoring Phase)
Spend 2 Assists. Choose a player in play with a 3PT Bonus and make a 3PT Shot Check at +1. If successful, immediately gain +2 Assists back.
Defensive Cards (Removing or Limiting Opponent Opportunities)
20. Box Out (Reaction Card)
Play immediately after your opponent wins a rebound.
Cancel your opponent’s rebound, and remove 1 additional rebound from their Rebound Track if your player has a Power advantage.
21. Smothering Defense (Reaction Card)
Play when an opponent declares a shot check.
If your defending player has a Defensive Bonus, reduce opponent's shot check bonus result by –3, down to a minimum of 0.
22. Denial (Reaction Card)
Discard a card.
After your opponent announces a shot check, force them to discard 2 Assists. If they don't have enough assists, their scoring roll suffers a –3 penalty instead.
24. Swarming Defense (Matchup Phase)
Choose the opposing player with the highest salary. Roll a die. 1-10, no result. 11-20, this player must roll their scoring twice, and take the lower result.
Special Plays (Creative and Tactical)
26. Rim Protector (Reaction Card)
If your defender’s Power + Defensive Boost totals 15 or higher, play when an opponent attempts a Paint Shot Check.
Reduce the Shot Check roll by 4, and if the shot is missed, your team immediately gains +2 Rebounds.
27. Defensive Anchor (Matchup Phase)
Select a defender with a Defensive Boost of +3 or greater.
All opposing players matched against this defender receive no positive roll bonuses from Speed or Power this period.
NBA Concepts & Sets
1. Spain Pick & Roll (Matchup Phase)
Choose a player with higher speed than their defender. They gain +2 to scoring rolls this period.
If they score on their roll, immediately add +1 Assist to your Assist Track.
2. Five-Out Offense (Scoring Phase)
Select a player with a 3PT Bonus.
They may attempt two 3PT Shot Checks instead of their normal roll, each at +1.
3. Drop Coverage (Reaction Card)
After your opponent announces their scoring player, if your defender has a Defensive Bonus, your opponent’s Paint Shot Check difficulty increases by +2 this roll.
4. Hammer Set (Scoring Phase)
If a player without a 3PT Bonus has a Speed advantage against their defender, they may attempt a 3PT Shot Check at normal difficulty.
If successful, add +2 Assists.
5. Short-Roll Playmaker (Matchup Phase)
Designate a player with at least 8 Power and 8 Speed as your short-roll facilitator.
This player adds +1 Assist whenever they successfully score in the paint this period.
Creative Rebound & Assist Strategies
6. Glass Cleaner (Reaction Card)
Play after an opponent misses any shot check.
Immediately add +2 to your Rebound Track. If the defending player has a Power advantage, add an additional +1.
7. Transition Outlet (Reaction Card)
Spend a rebound and assist, choose one player with Speed advantage against their defender.
Immediately roll a normal shot check at +2. If successful, add +1 Assist.
Leveraging Matchups (Speed/Power)
9. Mismatch Hunter (Matchup Phase)
Identify one player with at least a +4 Speed or Power advantage this period.
Their scoring rolls gain an additional +2 boost.
10. Strength in Numbers (Matchup Phase)
If all five of your players have at least a +1 advantage (Speed or Power) on offense this scoring phase, your team gains +3 Assists immediately.
11. Help Defender (Reaction Card)
If your defender has a Power and Speed disadvantage of –3 or worse, immediately switch this defender with another teammate not yet matched up, ignoring positional assignments for this period.   We need a help defender card but this shouldn’t be it, I don’t think.
Empowering Low-Salary Players
12. Unsung Hero (Scoring Phase)
Choose a player with a salary of 400 or less.
They roll two D20 dice, keeping the higher roll for their scoring chart this period.
13. Hustle Play (Reaction Card)
When an opposing player with salary above 800 attempts a shot check, your player with a salary below 400 may contest it, subtracting their defensive bonus from the opposing player’s shot check.
14. Energizer (Matchup Phase)
Play after matchup phase
Select a player with salary below 250.
They gain +3 Speed and +3 Power for defensive matchups only this scoring phase.
High Screen & Roll
Play during the matchup phase
Once at least two players from each team are matched up, choose one defender and switch his matchup with any other defender for 4-minutes.
Go Under
Play during the matchup phase
If the opposing manager played a screen and roll card this phase, negate the matchup switch. The opposing manager chooses one of the two players involved, and makes a three-point shot check at +2.
Fight Over
Play during the matchup phase
If the opposing manager played a screen and roll card this phase, negate the matchup switch. The offensive player with the higher speed of the two players involved rolls on their scoring chart at +2 for 4-minutes.
