# The Climb: starter kits and their "+" versions

**APPROVED 2026-10-19** with five changes (the user): Go Under's offence picks the
shooter again and the check is at −4; Spain Pick & Roll +4 keeping its +1 Assist;
Glass Cleaner +4 keeping +1 more; Pick Up Full Court −4 and 4 minutes; Cold Spell
natural 1-5 and −1.

2026-10-18. The Climb, piece 1, Task 1 (docs/plans/2026-10-18-the-climb.md).
Nothing here ships until the user approves it (the strategy card approval
rule). A run's deck is the **core** plus one **playbook's** eight; a Rest Day's
Practice turns **one copy** into its "+".

**SECOND DRAFT, 2026-10-19.** The first draft made each card one step
stronger; the user: "there are seldom upgrades that justify using a rest day
on them instead of resting the players. I think the upgrades should be a fair
amount stronger." **The rule now:** a "+" is clearly worth a Rest Day — about
half again to double the card's main number, or the card plus one rider the
game already has (an Assist, a card drawn, 🔒 Uncancelable, a drawback
removed, a cheaper cost). No new mechanics, no new targets.

These texts already name the faces on the Practice screen (Show practiced
versions); the effects are built once the user approves them.
